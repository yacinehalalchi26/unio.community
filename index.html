    What we stand for

    Welcome

    The door is open before you ask. In your first week someone knocks, not to sell you anything, but to say your name out loud. Nobody takes attendance, and the invitation never expires.

    Gather

    Belonging is a practice, not an amenity. Same table, same night, every week, whatever the weather. Residents host; we carry the chairs and get out of the way.

    Belong

    A building is just walls until someone knows your name. We measure ourselves by one question: do the people here feel known? When they do, they stay.

    Ensemble.

    One word, both our languages.

    Our philosophy

    In French, ensemble means together. In English, an ensemble is a group of musicians who only sound right when they play as one: no soloists, no audience, everyone carrying a part.

    That is what a building can be. Unio sets the tempo (Thursday, seven o'clock), hands out the parts (a welcome, a table, a few circles) and then steps back. The music is the neighbours. It always was.
